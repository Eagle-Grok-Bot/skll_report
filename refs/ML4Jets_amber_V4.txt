Motivation and physical picture
• JUNO: 20-kton liquid scintillator detector determining
  the Neutrino Mass Ordering

• Cosmic-muon-produced 9Li /8 He isotopes mimic the
  Inverse Beta Decay signal, limiting this physics
  sensitivity
• Muon track reconstruction for background suppression
  • TT: Multiple layers of plastic scintillator strips ->
    precisely detect subset muon tracks
  • WCD PMTs: Detects Cherenkov light -> muon hit
    patterns
  • CD PMTs: Detects scintillation light -> high-
    multiplicity muon hit patterns

Summary
• Fully data-driven training strategy using real TT-labeled events
• Amber-S and Amber-D
  • Best-performing WCD-based reconstruction method
  • Adopted in JUNO’s o cial reconstruction
  • Used in JUNO physics results presented at Neutrino 2026
• Amber-OCR shows promising performance on high-multiplicity CD PMT inputs
  • ∼ 5 × faster training
  • Further improved reconstruction accuracy
• Extending Amber to more reconstruction tasks and advanced architectures
